5 Unbounce Alternatives That Cost Less and Do More

Unbounce is a solid landing page builder. It's been around since 2009, has a mature drag-and-drop editor, and their Smart Traffic AI feature is genuinely useful. But here's the thing: at $99/month for the cheapest plan, it prices out a huge chunk of small businesses, freelancers, and early-stage startups.

If you're paying for Unbounce and wondering whether you're getting your money's worth — or shopping for a landing page builder and flinching at that price tag — you have options. Good ones. Here are five cheaper Unbounce alternatives that don't cut corners where it counts.

1. Leadpages — The Mainstream Pick ($37/mo)

Leadpages is the most obvious Unbounce alternative and probably what most people end up switching to. The Standard plan starts at $37/month billed annually ($49 monthly), which is less than half of Unbounce's entry price.

What you get for that price is surprisingly comprehensive: unlimited landing pages, unlimited traffic, pop-ups, alert bars, and a website builder thrown in. The template library has over 200 options, and they're sorted by conversion rate — a nice touch.

✓ Pros
  • 60% cheaper than Unbounce
  • Unlimited pages and traffic
  • Built-in payment processing
  • Excellent template variety
✗ Cons
  • A/B testing only on Pro plan ($74/mo)
  • Editor can feel rigid
  • No AMP page support

The honest take: If you're migrating from Unbounce and need a full-featured platform, Leadpages is the safest bet. You'll miss Unbounce's pixel-perfect editor control, but you'll save $62/month minimum.

2. Carrd — The $9/Year Wildcard

Carrd is almost absurdly cheap. The free tier gives you 3 sites. The Pro Lite plan is $9/year — not a typo, nine dollars per year — and Pro Plus maxes out at $49/year for 25 sites with custom domains.

The trade-off? Carrd builds one-page sites. There's no multi-page builder, no complex form logic, no A/B testing. But for a clean, fast landing page with a CTA? It's hard to beat. Indie hackers launching products on HTMLDecks or ProductHunt use Carrd all the time.

✓ Pros
  • Ridiculously affordable
  • Pages load instantly
  • Dead-simple UI
  • Great for MVPs and validation
✗ Cons
  • One-page sites only
  • No A/B testing
  • Limited integrations
  • Basic analytics

The honest take: Carrd is an Unbounce alternative the way a bicycle is a car alternative — it does the job differently and for far less money. Perfect for validation pages, coming-soon pages, and single CTAs.

3. Swipe Pages — Built for Mobile ($29/mo)

Swipe Pages is the underdog worth watching. Starting at $29/month, it's the only builder in this price range that offers AMP landing pages — Google's framework for near-instant mobile load times.

Why does that matter? If you're running paid ads (especially Google Ads), page speed directly impacts your Quality Score and cost-per-click. AMP pages load in under 2 seconds. That's not a marketing claim; it's the technical architecture.

✓ Pros
  • AMP pages on all plans
  • A/B testing included
  • Fast, modern editor
  • 70% cheaper than Unbounce
✗ Cons
  • Smaller template library
  • Traffic caps (20K-500K by plan)
  • Overage fees ($5/5K extra visits)

The honest take: If mobile ad campaigns are your bread and butter, Swipe Pages punches way above its weight. The AMP support alone justifies the switch from Unbounce for many paid acquisition teams.

4. Landingi — Template Library Beast ($29/mo)

Landingi has over 400 templates — easily the largest library in this comparison. Starting at $29/month, you get unlimited landing pages, pop-ups, and a solid drag-and-drop editor.

Where Landingi really shines is for agencies and teams managing multiple clients. The Professional plan ($49/mo) adds sub-accounts, audit logs, and white-labeling. Compare that to Unbounce's Agency plan at $499/month and you see the value gap.

✓ Pros
  • 400+ templates
  • Agency features at fraction of Unbounce's price
  • EventTracker built-in
  • Smart Sections for reusability
✗ Cons
  • UI feels slightly dated
  • Traffic-based pricing can add up
  • No native AMP support

The honest take: If you're an agency running landing pages for clients, Landingi gives you 80% of Unbounce's capability at 6% of the agency-tier price. That math speaks for itself.
