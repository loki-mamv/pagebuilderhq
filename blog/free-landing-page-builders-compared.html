Free Landing Page Builders Compared: What You Actually Get (And What's Locked)

"Free" is the most abused word in SaaS marketing. Every landing page builder claims a free plan or free trial, but the gap between "free to sign up" and "free to actually use" is enormous.

We signed up for every free landing page builder worth considering and documented exactly what you get without paying. The goal: help you figure out which free tier is genuinely usable and which is just a demo with a credit card form waiting around the corner.

1. Carrd — Best Truly Free Tier

What's Free

FREE Basic templates

Upgrade cost: $9/year (Pro Lite) to $49/year (Pro Plus)

Honest assessment: Carrd's free tier is the most usable on this list. You can build 3 complete, responsive one-page sites with no time limit. The Carrd branding is subtle (a small badge in the corner), and the designs look genuinely good.

The free tier doesn't include forms, which is a significant limitation for a landing page — you can't capture leads without paying. But at $9/year for Pro Lite, this is the cheapest upgrade in the entire landing page market.

Who it's best for: Quick validation pages, coming-soon sites, personal portfolios, simple product pages.

FREE Live chat
FREE CRM integration
PAID Remove HubSpot branding
PAID Custom domain

Upgrade cost: $20/mo (Starter) to $890/mo (Professional)

Honest assessment: On paper, HubSpot's free tier looks incredible — 30 landing pages, forms, live chat, AND a free CRM. In practice, there are two problems:

  1. HubSpot branding everywhere. Your pages will display HubSpot branding that you cannot remove without paying. For a business landing page, this looks unprofessional.
  2. The upsell gravity. HubSpot's free tier is a gateway into their ecosystem. Every feature you click leads to "upgrade to unlock." The free CRM collects your leads, and then HubSpot's sales team markets to you. It works — but know what you're signing up for.

Who it's best for: Small businesses already considering HubSpot's paid plans who want to test the platform before committing.

3. Mailchimp — Free Landing Pages (If You Don't Mind the Limits)

What's Free

FREE Unlimited landing pages
FREE Sign-up forms
FREE Basic templates
FREE 500 contacts
PAID Custom domains
PAID Remove branding
PAID Email automations
PAID A/B testing

Upgrade cost: $13/mo (Essentials) to $350/mo (Premium)

Honest assessment: Mailchimp's free landing pages are designed to feed leads into their email platform — that's the entire business model. The pages themselves are basic but functional. You get unlimited pages with sign-up forms.

The catch: the free plan limits you to 500 contacts and 1,000 email sends per month. The landing pages drive sign-ups that quickly push you into a paid plan. Mailchimp also recently removed free email automations from the free tier, making the landing pages less useful as standalone tools.

Who it's best for: Anyone who's going to use Mailchimp for email anyway. The landing pages are a bonus, not the main product.

4. Kit (formerly ConvertKit) — Best Free for Creators

What's Free

FREE Unlimited landing pages
FREE Unlimited email sends
FREE Up to 10,000 subscribers
FREE One email sequence
PAID Visual automations
PAID Third-party integrations
PAID Advanced reporting
LIMITED Template designs

Upgrade cost: $25/mo (Creator) to $50/mo (Creator Pro)

Honest assessment: Kit's free tier is surprisingly generous — 10,000 subscribers with unlimited email sends is unheard of. The landing pages are simple (think: email capture + hero section), but they work. No Kit branding on the pages, which is unusual for a free tier.

The landing page designs are minimal. If you want a feature-rich, visually impressive landing page, Kit isn't the right tool. But for creators building an email list, the landing page is just the front door — and Kit's free plan gives you the whole house.

Who it's best for: Content creators, newsletter operators, and anyone whose primary goal is growing an email list.

5. Google Sites — Actually 100% Free (But You'll Pay in Design)

What's Free

FREE Unlimited sites
FREE Custom domain
FREE No branding
FREE Google integrations
FREE Responsive design
FREE Unlimited storage
N/A No templates to speak of
N/A No conversion tools

Upgrade cost: Nothing. It's completely free with a Google account.

Honest assessment: Google Sites is the only tool on this list with zero upsell. It's genuinely, completely free. Custom domains, no branding, unlimited sites.

The problem: it looks like it was designed in 2015 and hasn't been updated since. The design options are extremely limited. There are no conversion-focused features — no pop-ups, no A/B testing, no analytics beyond basic Google Analytics integration. For a professional landing page, it simply doesn't cut it.

Who it's best for: Internal pages, documentation, or situations where design genuinely doesn't matter.

6. Wix — Generous Free Plan, Aggressive Ads

What's Free

FREE 500MB storage
FREE 500MB bandwidth
PAID Remove Wix ads
PAID Custom domain
PAID Analytics
PAID E-commerce

Upgrade cost: $17/mo (Light) to $36/mo (Business)

Honest assessment: Wix has one of the best drag-and-drop editors in the industry — the design experience is genuinely excellent. On the free plan, you can build a beautiful page.

The dealbreaker: Wix displays their ads on your free site. Not a small badge — actual banner ads. For a business landing page, this is a non-starter. You're driving paid traffic to a page that advertises Wix. That's not a landing page; that's a billboard for someone else.

Who it's best for: Prototyping designs before committing to a paid plan. Don't launch a business landing page on Wix's free tier.

The Bottom Line on Free Landing Page Builders

"Free" in landing page builders usually means one of three things:

  1. Truly free with design limitations (Google Sites, Kit)
  2. Free-ish with branding/upsells (HubSpot, Mailchimp, Wix)
  3. Free templates you host yourself (PageBuilderHQ)

None of these are bad — they're just different trade-offs. The worst outcome is spending 8 hours testing every free tier only to end up paying for a tool anyway. Pick the one that matches your skills and needs, and ship your page today.
