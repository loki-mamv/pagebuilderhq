## FAQ

### Can I use my own domain?
Yes. Point your domain to us and we handle SSL automatically. Takes about 5 minutes to set up.

### What happens if I cancel?
Your pages stay live for 30 days after cancellation. You can export your content anytime. It's your data.

### Do I need to know how to code?
No. Fill in the form fields — your headline, description, images, CTA — and we handle the design and code. If you do know code, the Scale plan lets you inject custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

### How fast are the pages?
Very. We generate clean, static HTML served from a global CDN. Sub-second load times, perfect Lighthouse scores. No bloated JavaScript, no unnecessary tracking scripts.

### Can I add forms and collect submissions?
Yes. Every page includes a built-in form. Launch plan includes 100 submissions per month, Scale plan includes unlimited. Submissions are emailed to you and stored in your dashboard.
